About

Honest scope. Real numbers. No vendor agenda.

Identity decisions are expensive to get wrong and slow to unwind. Our job is to make sure you make them once, with the right information, at the right time.

We lead with a diagnostic, not a proposal. We recommend platforms we do not resell. We put a price on every engagement before it starts. And when the right answer is "you do not need us for this," we say so.

Working cost-modeling software

Before this practice existed, we built a toolkit that models identity spend by headcount and volume across six cost categories. Our second opinion comes with a model attached.

Operating experience, not just advisory experience

We have run these programs, hired for these teams, and sat through these renewals. The advice reflects what works in practice.

Vendor-neutral, always

No reseller margins, no referral fees from the platforms we evaluate. When we score Okta against Entra, the only weight on the scale is your requirements.
